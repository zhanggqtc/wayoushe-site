一句话结论：One-line conclusion: 把高校特权从「固定 3 款/月」扩为「N 款自选池（推荐 12 款）+ 每日随机 3 款 + 锁定做任务转正」——发放量不变，蚕食增量 ≈ 0。Expand Campus Privilege from "fixed 3 skins/month" to "an N-skin self-selection pool (recommended 12) + 3 random daily + lock-and-complete-tasks-to-convert" — total grant volume unchanged, incremental cannibalization ≈ 0.
关键边界（看方案先读这条）：Key boundary (read first): 特权自拟池仅覆盖 se/de 那一批老冷皮；轮转里卖得好的高级皮肤（热门/高价值款）不在池内，照常全价售卖、零蚕食。即特权影响的只是轮转长尾一小撮，与高流水高级皮无关。The privilege self-selection pool only covers the legacy low-tier (se/de) skins; the well-selling premium skins in rotation (popular/high-value) are NOT in the pool, sold at full price as usual with zero cannibalization. In other words, privilege only affects a tiny long-tail slice of rotation, unrelated to high-revenue premium skins.

一、改版机制：从「固定 3 款」到「池内每日随机 + 锁定养成」I. Revamp Mechanism: from "Fixed 3" to "Daily Random in Pool + Lock-to-Cultivate"

① 每月建池① Build pool monthly官方配置 N 款 2~3 年前经典皮肤入自选池Ops configures N classic skins from 2–3 years ago into the self-selection pool
→
② 每日随机 3 款② 3 random daily玩家每天从池内随机刷出 3 款可见Players see 3 randomly drawn skins from the pool each day
→
③ 喜欢即锁定③ Lock if you like遇到心仪款可锁定（最多锁 3 款）Lock a favorite skin when found (up to 3)
→
④ 活跃任务转正④ Convert via tasks完成活跃任务后，锁定款转为下月特权皮肤After activity tasks, locked skins become next month's privilege skins

核心变化：发放量不变（仍 3 款/人/月），但「给什么」从系统定死 → 玩家自选。新增「每日回访看池」+「锁定后做任务」两个活跃钩子。Core change: Grant volume unchanged (still 3 skins/user/month), but WHAT is given shifts from system-fixed → player-chosen. Adds two engagement hooks: "daily return to check pool" + "complete tasks after locking".

二、预设结论 ①：自选池规模建议 12 款（区间 10~15）II. Preset Conclusion ①: Recommended pool size 12 (range 10–15)

下限 10：随机性才有意义Lower bound 10: Randomness only matters

C(10,3)=120
每日 3 款组合数 ≥120，30 天内体验不重复；低于 10 款则"随机"流于形式Daily 3-skin combinations ≥120, no repeat within 30 days; below 10, "random" becomes nominal

推荐 12：曝光与选择成本平衡Recommended 12: Balanced exposure & cost

上限 15：超过则体验衰减Upper bound 15: Diminishing beyond

<20%
单款日曝光率 <20%，刷不到喜欢的款挫败感上升；且符合条件的老冷皮供给本身有限Single-skin daily exposure <20%, frustration rises when favorites aren't drawn; also supply of eligible legacy skins is limited
为什么不是越大越好：Why not "bigger is better": 池子扩大不抬高发放天花板（仍 3 款/月），只提升"遇到喜欢款的概率"。该概率在 10→15 区间边际收益最高；继续扩大只会增加选择疲劳与配置/审核成本，对特权价值贡献趋平。Enlarging the pool does NOT raise the grant ceiling (still 3/month); it only raises the "probability of meeting a favorite". That probability has the highest marginal return in the 10→15 range; further enlargement only adds choice fatigue and config/review cost, with flat contribution to privilege value.

三、预设结论 ②：自选对特权价值的贡献（框架 + 待验证）III. Preset Conclusion ②: Self-Selection's Contribution to Privilege Value (framework + pending validation)

价值逻辑：Value logic: 特权感知价值 ≈ 命中率 × 拥有量。拥有量不变（3 款/月），自选把「命中率」（拿到的 3 款里有自己喜欢的）从"被动接受"拉到"主动锁定"，预期命中率从 ~30% 提升至 ~80%+（预设值，待用研标定）。Perceived privilege value ≈ hit-rate × ownership. Ownership unchanged (3/month); self-selection lifts "hit-rate" (among the 3 received, how many are liked) from "passive acceptance" to "active locking", expected hit-rate from ~30% to ~80%+ (preset value, pending research calibration).
行为增益：Behavioral gain: 「每日随机看池」制造每日打开理由，「锁定后做活跃任务」把特权从"领了就走"变成"持续活跃"，直接作用于特权用户留存与活跃时长。"Daily random pool check" creates a daily open reason; "complete tasks after locking" turns privilege from "grab-and-leave" into "sustained engagement", directly improving privilege-user retention and active duration.
收入风险 ≈ 0 增量：Revenue risk ≈ 0 incremental: 蚕食天花板由"每月白给 3 款"决定，与池子大小无关；标的仅 10~15 款 se/de 级老冷皮，相对全游戏月流水 13~15 亿元，最坏影响在个位数 % 以内，改版本身增量 ≈ 0。The cannibalization ceiling is set by "3 free skins/month", unrelated to pool size; the targets are only 10–15 se/de legacy skins; vs. the game's monthly revenue of ¥1.3–1.5B, worst-case impact is within single-digit %, so the revamp's own increment ≈ 0.
蚕食范围边界：Cannibalization boundary: 特权自拟池仅覆盖 se/de 那一批老冷皮；轮转中卖得好的高级皮肤（热门/高价值款）不在池内，照常全价售卖、与特权零交集，不受任何蚕食。即特权影响的只是轮转长尾里的一小撮，与高流水高级皮无关。The privilege self-selection pool only covers the se/de legacy skins; well-selling premium skins in rotation (popular/high-value) are NOT in the pool, sold at full price with zero overlap with privilege, hence no cannibalization. Privilege only affects a tiny long-tail slice of rotation, unrelated to high-revenue premium skins.

调研一句话：One-line: 存量高校特权用户强烈支持提高轮换频率（每日/每周变化），愿为此更常打开游戏、完成活跃任务；但"给什么"须升级为热门枪型 + 更好品质（特效/不丑），否则高频难转化满意度。Existing Campus Privilege users strongly support increasing rotation frequency (daily/weekly changes), willing to open the game more and complete tasks for it; but WHAT is given must upgrade to popular gun types + better quality (effects/not ugly), otherwise high frequency alone won't convert to satisfaction.
① 轮换获强支持：① Rotation strongly supported: Q7「每天变化更常打开」均值 8.24/10（54% 满分）；Q8「愿为更新枪皮做任务」均值 7.49/10（48% 满分）。Q7 "daily changes make me open more" mean 8.24/10 (54% full marks); Q8 "willing to do tasks for updated gun skins" mean 7.49/10 (48% full marks).
② 活跃增益扎实：② Solid engagement gain: Q7 ≥8 分占 71.5%、Q8 ≥8 分占 60.3%，印证"打开理由 + 任务钩子"。Q7 ≥8 accounts for 71.5%, Q8 ≥8 for 60.3%, confirming "open reason + task hook".
③ 内容须升级：③ Content must upgrade: 89.8% 偏好热门枪型；开放题高频要"特效皮肤"、嫌"贴图皮丑"；但满意度仅 6.40/10、28.6% 不关注轮换皮肤。89.8% prefer popular gun types; open-ended answers frequently ask for "effect skins" and complain "sticker skins are ugly"; but satisfaction is only 6.40/10, 28.6% don't care about rotation skins.
④ 边界需重估（关键）：④ Boundary needs re-evaluation (key): 玩家要"热门枪型+特效"，与原方案「仅 se/de 老冷皮入池、保护高价值皮零蚕食」有张力。若只给老冷皮/贴图皮仍会被嫌丑；若要满足特效/热门，蚕食边界须 DA 复核后再定清单。Players want "popular guns + effects", which tensions with the original plan "only se/de legacy skins in pool, zero cannibalization of high-value skins". If only legacy/sticker skins are given, they'll still be called ugly; if effects/popular are to be satisfied, the cannibalization boundary must be re-checked via DA before finalizing the list.
⑤ 缺未开通样本：⑤ Missing unactivated sample: 94.9% 已开通、仅 5 人未开通 → 本卷只能证明对存量用户的活跃/满意增益，无法证明拉新/开通意愿，需补投未开通学生样本。94.9% already activated, only 5 not → this survey only proves engagement/satisfaction gain for existing users, cannot prove acquisition/activation willingness; need to supplement with unactivated student sample.
