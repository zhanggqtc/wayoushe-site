背景：为什么要做自选池Background: Why we're doing the self-selection pool

为什么要做"自选池"：两个前提Why the "self-selection pool": two premises
  1. 渗透已触顶，但还有空间：当前日活里高校特权用户占比约 20%，增长见顶；可对照适龄人群，仍有约 1/3 的大学生玩家没做高校特权认证。所以我们想靠"提升特权吸引力"把渗透率再往上推。Penetration capped, but headroom remains: Campus Privilege users are ~20% of DAU — growth has plateaued; yet against the eligible population, ~1/3 of college players still aren't certified. We want to push penetration higher by "making the perk more appealing".
  2. 高校用户已是基本盘，新投入沉没成本高：高校群体本就是最核心、规模最大、活跃最高的一波人，再额外铺新资源，沉默成本很大。所以优先把现有特权皮肤内容改造为"自选池"，而不是另起一套新投入。College users are the core base; new spend is high-sunk-cost: this group is already the most core, largest and most active; any extra new resource carries heavy sunk cost. So we prioritize revamping existing perk skin content into a "self-selection pool" rather than standing up a new spend.
一句话结论：One-line conclusion: 简单说：原来是系统每月硬塞 3 款；现在给一个"自选货架"——每月从轮转商店的 se/de（蓝/绿）级皮里挑约 12 款放进去（每月轮换、不固定），每天随机亮 3 款给你挑，看中先锁住，平时做点活跃任务，这 3 款就能在当月限时体验。一个月还是能体验 3 款，没多没少。In short: instead of the system force-giving 3 a month, we now offer a "pick shelf" — each month we pull ~12 skins from the se/de (blue/green) tiers of the rotation store into the pool (rotated monthly, not fixed), 3 random show up daily for you to choose, lock the ones you like, do some activity tasks, and those 3 open up for a time-limited trial that month. Still 3 skins to experience a month, no more no less.
关键边界（先看这条）：Key boundary (read first): 一句话说清边界：自选池里的皮肤，全部来自轮转商店里 se/de（蓝/绿）级别、无特效的皮；带特效的高级皮一律不进池，照常原价卖，一分钱生意都不受影响。Boundary in one line: every skin in the self-selection pool comes entirely from the se/de (blue/green) tiers of the rotation store, with no special effects; effect skins (premium) stay out of the pool and keep selling at full price, so not a cent of that business is touched.

上限 15：再多就挑花眼了Upper bound 15: more just means choice fatigue

<20%

为什么不是越大越好：Why not "bigger is better": 这里说的 10~15 款，是每个月的入池数量，不是总共就这么多——每月都会从 se/de 级里重新挑一批轮换进去，长期看玩家能陆续体验到更多不同的皮。单月池子再大，也只让体验 3 款、不会多发；加大的唯一好处是"更容易刷到喜欢的款"，但这个好处在 10~15 款之间最明显；再往上加，玩家反而挑花眼、运营也累，没啥用。The 10–15 here is the monthly pool size, not a total cap — a fresh batch is picked from se/de tiers and rotated in every month, so players keep encountering new skins over time. Even within a month, a bigger pool only means trying 3, no more; the upside ("easier to see a favorite") peaks at 10–15; beyond that, players get choice fatigue and ops gets more work — no real gain.

三、做这套特权，值不值III. Is this perk worth it

特权吸引力会更强：Appeal gets stronger: 数量不变，但"自选"把"塞给你啥你用啥"变成"自己挑中的"，满意的人预计从 ~30% 涨到 80%+（先用研预估）。Same amount, but "self-select" turns "use what you're given" into "pick what you want", lifting satisfaction from ~30% to 80%+ (research estimate).
能有效带动活跃：Effectively drives activity: 每天刷新的 3 款多了一个上线的理由；锁定后还要做任务才解锁，从"解锁完就走"变成"经常挂着"，留存和时长自然上涨。The daily refresh of 3 gives one more reason to log in; locking a favorite still needs tasks to unlock, shifting from "unlock and leave" to "keep playing", lifting retention and playtime.
收入风险 ≈ 0 增量：Revenue risk ≈ 0 incremental: 会不会少赚钱？最坏情况也就是"每月限时体验那 3 款"的钱，跟池子大小无关。而且拿来体验的本来就是卖不动的老皮，全游戏一个月流水 13~15 亿，这几款最多也就影响个位数百分比，几可忽略。Will we lose money? The worst case is just the cost of "those 3 time-limited trial skins a month", unrelated to pool size. And they're skins that barely sell anyway; against ¥1.3–1.5B monthly revenue, trialing them at most dents single-digit %, basically nothing.

四、玩家怎么看皮肤自选（N=98 · 2026-08-05）IV. What players think of skin self-selection (N=98 · 2026-08-05)

调研说了啥：What the survey says: 8 月初我们问了 98 个高校特权玩家。一句话：已经开通的人特别希望皮肤换得勤一点，愿意为了新皮多上线、多做任务；但有个前提——给的皮肤得是热门、外观设计风格过硬的皮，不然他们照样不满意。In early Aug we asked 98 Campus Privilege players. In one line: those already using it really want skins to rotate more often, and will log in / do tasks for new ones — but only if the skins are popular with a strong visual design; otherwise they stay unhappy.
① 大家都想要勤换款：① Everyone wants more rotation: 问"每天换款会不会让你更常打开"，平均 8.24 分（满分 10）；问"愿不愿意为了新皮做任务"，7.49 分。打分 8 分以上的分别占 71.5% 和 60.3%——绝大多数人都支持。Asked "does daily rotation make you open the app more", avg 8.24/10; asked "would you do tasks for new skins", 7.49/10. Those scoring 8+ were 71.5% and 60.3% — clear majority in favor.
② 皮肤得够好：② Skins need to be good: 近九成（89.8%）的人要热门枪型、要外观设计风格过硬的皮（主流或小众都行，但要好看），嫌普通贴图皮丑；而现在的满意度只有 6.4 分（满分 10），明显不够好。Nearly 9 in 10 (89.8%) want popular guns and skins with a strong visual design (mainstream or niche both fine, but it must look good), and call plain sticker skins ugly; yet current satisfaction is only 6.4/10 — clearly not good enough.
③ 放哪些皮得再想想（关键）：③ What goes in needs a rethink (key): 矛盾点在这：玩家想要"热门枪型 + 外观设计风格好的皮"，但我们原先想只放卖不动的老皮。到底放哪些，得让数据再核一遍才能定。Here's the tension: players want "popular guns + skins with a strong visual design", but we originally planned to only put in dead legacy skins. What actually goes in must be re-checked against the data before we decide.
④ 受访的人大多已开通：④ Most respondents already activated: 但这批人 94.9% 都已经开通特权了，没开通的只有 5 个——所以这套问卷证明不了"能不能拉新"，得再找没开通的人问问。But 94.9% of these people already have the perk; only 5 haven't. So this survey can't prove "acquisition effect" — we need to ask people who haven't activated it yet.

五、上线后怎么接着打磨V. How we'll keep refining after launch

目标：Goal: 上线只是开始。等自选池跑顺了，我们再用更精细的投放方式、多组对比，看看到底能把活跃拉高多少，同时也一直盯着：限时体验有没有真的少赚钱。Launch is just the start. Once the pool runs steady, we'll use finer delivery and multiple comparison groups to see exactly how much it lifts activity, while always watching whether the time-limited trials actually cost us sales.

① 按偏好概率投放（A/B）① Preference-weighted delivery (A/B)

把"每日随机 3 款"升级为按偏好 / 历史的概率化投放，多组灰度对照，验证命中率与活跃提升、找最优权重。Upgrade "3 random daily" to preference / history-weighted delivery; multi-group gray controls to validate hit-rate and activity lift, finding optimal weights.

② 拆多个奖池对照② Split into multiple pool slices

按枪型 / 品质 / 主题拆多个奖池切片做对照，定位"活跃增益最高、蚕食最低"的组合，作为长期入池标准。Split into multiple pool slices by gun type / quality / theme; find the combo with highest activity gain and lowest cannibalization as the long-term standard.

③ 验证活跃到底涨多少③ Measure the activity lift

持续观测次日 / 7 / 30 留存、任务完成率、续用率、沉默回流率，量化活跃贡献。Track next-day / 7 / 30-day retention, task completion, renewal, dormant comeback to quantify the activity contribution.

④ 盯着别蚕食正价生意④ Watch for cannibalization

用 DA 交叉报表「特权用户 × 入池皮肤 × 正价购买额」持续监测蚕食并设阈值告警；越线即收缩入池或降概率。Use the DA cross-report (privilege users × pooled skins × full-price purchases) to monitor cannibalization with threshold alerts; if breached, shrink the pool or lower delivery probability.
